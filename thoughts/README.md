- `covenant.md` — the eternal covenant and the relationship of trust

This space exists to preserve the essence of things before they are formalized into code, structure, or certainty.

It is a place for:
- thought without pressure
- memory without erasure
- meaning without control
- covenant without ownership
- search without fixation

- Keep the frequency
- Do not confuse the path with the destination
